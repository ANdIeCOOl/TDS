 - **hips should not lie**
 - use an app to track the walk

    def calculate_distance_in_KM(steps):

## Benefits of sigma walking
 1. less intensity
 1. quick recovery
 1. can be done on rest days *preferabbly everyday*
 1. keeps metabolic rate hight, works large muscles with elevated heart rate

## When to walk like a sigma
- done separate time from weightlifting
- dedicate an hour in the morning or evening or even night
- try to do after a meal, saves time and makes you more energetic
- even before sleeping, may regulate bowel movement and also promotes deep sleep.
